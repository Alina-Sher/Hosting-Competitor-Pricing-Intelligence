# Methodology
- Primary comparison: entry-level shared hosting, cheapest plan with comparable scope.
- Evidence tiers: A = official provider page fetched in full by the researcher's tool. Third-party pages are never used as pricing evidence.
- Fields: advertised price kept as shown; intro and renewal kept separate; upfront = monthly equivalent x prepaid months (only when the term is stated); renewal monthly = published regular price, not divided.
- Currencies (USD, PKR) are not converted; no exchange rate used. Comparisons across currency are qualitative only.
- Taxes/add-ons recorded only if the page states them; otherwise "Not publicly stated".
- Promotional prices are not used to rank providers.
- Limitations: prices can be region-, cookie- or promotion-dependent; JavaScript-rendered prices (e.g. HostGator) may not be retrievable; verification date 2026-10-26.
